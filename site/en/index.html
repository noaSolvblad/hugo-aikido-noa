MORE THAN MARTIAL ARTS

BUILD YOUR CONFIDENCE.
BECOME ONE OF US.

You do not need to be strong to start. You just need a willingness to learn.

Aiki-do is one of Denmark’s oldest active ju-jitsu clubs. Rooted on Amager, we have practised self-defence since 1965. We come together to train, refine our technique and enjoy making progress together.

Whether you are completely new or have trained before, you are welcome on the mat. Bring your training clothes and give ju-jitsu a try.
